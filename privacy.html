Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Chickadee is voice & AI for Home Assistant. Its privacy posture depends entirely on which engines you point it at — three modes, three answers. Everything described here is verifiable in the open-source repository. Chickadee is built and operated by the makers of Dashie; accounts and billing run on the same backend (documented in PROVENANCE.md).

What leaves your box, per mode

Local mode (your own engines, no account)

Nothing leaves your network. Speech goes to the STT/TTS/LLM endpoints you configured (your Ollama box, your Whisper server), the assistant runs inside the add-on, and no Chickadee service is contacted — there is no account, no telemetry, no version ping.

Bring-your-own-key

Audio and text go to the providers you configured (for example Google, OpenAI, OpenRouter) under your API key, directly from your box. Chickadee's servers are not involved. Your keys are stored on-box only (/data/api-keys.json, file mode 600), are masked in the console UI, and are excluded from Home Assistant backups.

Chickadee Cloud (signed in)

When you leave an engine blank while signed in, that stage runs on hosted engines under your account:

  • Sent per turn: the audio (for speech-to-text), the turn text plus your Assist-exposed entity context (for the AI), and the reply text (for text-to-speech). Context is scoped to what the question needs — Chickadee sends your exposed-entity states, not your whole Home Assistant config.
  • Stored for billing: per-call usage rows — model, token counts, latency, cost. Metered credits require this; it's the invoice.
  • Transcripts: stored only if you enable transcript retention / "share to improve" in the console (off by default). Without it, billing rows carry usage numbers, not your words.

Account sign-in & Google data

Chickadee Cloud uses Google Sign-In solely to identify your account. We request only these scopes:

  • openid, email, profile — your basic profile (name, picture) and email address, for account identification and billing.

Chickadee never requests access to your Google Calendar, Google Drive, or any other Google data. We do not store your Google password; authentication is handled entirely through Google's OAuth 2.0 flow. You can revoke Chickadee's access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.

Service providers

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. To operate Chickadee Cloud we rely on a small set of trusted processors:

  • Supabase — account records, settings, and billing/usage data (PostgreSQL, encrypted at rest).
  • Stripe — payment processing for credit purchases. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout and handled by Stripe; Chickadee never sees or stores your card number.
  • Hosted AI & voice providers — for stages you run on Chickadee Cloud, audio/text is sent to the underlying speech and language providers under our enterprise/API accounts, whose terms prohibit using submitted data to train or improve their models.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

Data storage and security

  • Encryption in transit: all data between your device, our servers, and provider APIs uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest: account and billing data stored in Supabase is encrypted at rest.
  • Access tokens: OAuth tokens are stored securely and are never logged or exposed in application code.
  • On-box keys: in bring-your-own-key mode, your provider keys never leave your Home Assistant box.
  • Secure infrastructure: our backend runs on Supabase and Vercel, both SOC 2 Type II certified platforms.

Data retention and deletion

  • Billing/usage data: per-call usage rows are retained as the record of your metered credit usage (your invoice history).
  • Transcripts: retained only if you opted in; can be turned off at any time in the console.
  • Account deletion: available in the console (Account → Danger Zone). Billing stops immediately, OAuth tokens are revoked, and your data is purged after a 15-day grace window.

Artificial intelligence and machine learning

Chickadee does not train its own AI/ML models on your data, and does not use, retain, or transfer your data — including Google profile/email data — to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized models, whether our own or any third party's. Where Chickadee Cloud uses hosted AI, we rely on the enterprise/API tiers of each provider, whose terms contractually prohibit using customer-submitted data for training. No data is sent to any AI provider except to fulfill a request you initiated, and only the minimum data required.

Things Chickadee never does, in any mode

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Sentry, PostHog, …).
  • No startup phone-home, no update checks, no version pings.
  • No listening: wake-word detection is your satellite's job; the add-on only receives the audio Home Assistant's Assist pipeline hands it.
  • The LAN engine scan (console → Local Engines → Scan) runs only when you click it, probes private subnets only, uses read-only unauthenticated version-endpoint requests to identify engines, and its results render in the console — they are never sent off-box.
  • One caveat for completeness: the console page in your browser loads a few pinned open-source libraries from a public CDN — a standard static fetch by your browser, no data sent beyond the request itself. The voice pipeline never touches a CDN.
  • The add-on ↔ integration bridge is same-box only (nothing is exposed on your LAN), authenticated with a random per-install secret.

Google API Limited Use

Chickadee's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data (email and profile) only for authentication and account identification. We do not use Google data for advertising, do not sell it, do not allow humans to read it except where you explicitly request support or where required for security or law, and do not use or transfer it to train generalized or non-personalized AI/ML models.

Children's privacy

Chickadee is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be communicated to signed-in users.

Contact

Privacy questions are welcome in public — open an issue on the Chickadee repository. You can also email hello@getchickadee.org. Chickadee is operated by Dashie LLC.
