Why Chickadee Voice

Three things that set it apart from raw Home Assistant, Alexa, or Google.

Plug & Play

Chickadee Voice comes pre-configured with access to web search, sports scores, weather, Home Assistant entities, and more. Fully configurable to use a curated selection of local or cloud-based tools to deliver AI your whole household can use.

Adaptive context

Chickadee sends the AI only what your question needs — the relevant devices and data, not your whole house. That's faster, cheaper, and more accurate.

Privacy & Your Own Model

Claude, Gemini, Bedrock, or Ollama running locally — you pick the LLM. Cloud queries are anonymized through Chickadee. No Google, Amazon, Anthropic, or OpenAI account. No lock-in.

You decide what leaves your home

Every part of the pipeline is your choice — and Chickadee tells you, in plain color, whether a choice is local or cloud.

Local (green)

  • Run your own LLM — your home and device data never leaves the network
  • On-device speech and local text-to-speech for a fully offline stack
  • Device control always runs locally — works with no internet
  • No account and no ongoing cost — you are only paying for your own hardware

Cloud (orange)

  • Cloud-based LLMs can be more economical than high-powered hardware for a local LLM
  • Advanced speech recognition and character voices
  • Web search and live data tools (sports, weather, places)
  • Queries aggregated and account-anonymized through Chickadee
  • Draws on your credit balance — metered and transparent
When you choose Chickadee Voice, we show a one-time privacy summary and color-code each component. Pick a local model alongside local speech and voices and nothing but optional web lookups ever leaves your LAN.
