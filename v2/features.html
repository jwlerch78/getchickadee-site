Screensaver & Photos

Photo slideshows from multiple sources with motion-activated wake

Beautiful when idle, ready when you need it

When your tablet isn't in use, the screensaver takes over with a photo slideshow, a clock overlay, and current weather. Pull photos from your self-hosted Immich library, Google Drive, Unsplash for curated backgrounds, or Home Assistant's My Media for local files.

Switch between sources on the fly, choose specific albums or folders, and let Chickadee rotate through your collection automatically. Walk up and the tablet wakes instantly — Chickadee can use the camera for motion or face detection so your dashboard is ready before you even touch the screen.

Voice Control

Voice commands with a custom wake word

Say "Chickadee" and take control

Turn any tablet into a voice-controlled smart home hub. Chickadee's custom-trained wake word runs entirely on-device — no cloud listening, no always-on microphone streaming. When you say "Chickadee," your command is processed locally using natural language understanding, then routed to Home Assistant to execute. Chickadee also supports other wake words such as "Alexa" and "Hey Jarvis."

Control lights, run scenes, check the weather, set timers, and more. Chickadee speaks responses back, and you can choose whether responses are read aloud, displayed on screen, or both.

Lock Mode

Keep your tablet locked down for kiosk use

Set it and forget it

Lock Mode keeps your tablet dedicated to its dashboard. It prevents users from accidentally exiting the app, navigating to other apps, or changing settings. Protect it with an optional PIN code so only authorized users can unlock.

Combined with Android's Device Owner mode, Chickadee can fully lock the tablet to a single-app kiosk experience — perfect for wall-mounted panels and shared family displays.

Music Player

Multi-room audio powered by Music Assistant

Music everywhere, controlled from anywhere

Chickadee integrates with Music Assistant to turn your tablets into a multi-room audio system. Play music from Spotify, YouTube Music, or your local library — and sync playback across every Chickadee tablet in your home.

Control playback from the sidebar, via voice ("Chickadee, play jazz in the kitchen"), or through Home Assistant automations. Each tablet is a speaker, a controller, or both.

Video Streaming

Live camera feeds and Frigate recording playback on your dashboard

Keep an eye on every room

Bring your security cameras right onto the dashboard. Chickadee shows live feeds from any RTSP, go2rtc, or Home Assistant camera — docked in a sidebar strip or floating over your dashboard. Have a feed pop up automatically when motion is detected or a door opens, with an optional chime, and choose how each tablet in your home reacts.

For Frigate cameras, Chickadee adds a full recording timeline. Scrub back through the day, replay detected events from a thumbnail list, and jump back to live with a tap — all without leaving your dashboard. You can even turn a spare Chickadee tablet into a camera the rest of your home can watch.

Battery Management

Built-in charge control for always-on tablets

Keep your tablet healthy, permanently plugged in

Wall-mounted tablets live on the charger 24/7, which degrades battery life fast. Chickadee's built-in battery management solves this by automatically cycling the charge between configurable thresholds — no Home Assistant automations or smart plugs required.

Set your preferred charge range (e.g., 20%–80%) and Chickadee handles the rest. Monitor battery health and charging status right from the Control Center.

Home Assistant Integration

Dashboard hosting, sidebar control, and a Fully Kiosk-compatible API

Your dashboards, front and center

Connect Chickadee to your Home Assistant instance and display any dashboard in a clean, full-screen kiosk view. Hide the HA sidebar and tab bar for a distraction-free experience, or keep them visible for quick navigation.

Chickadee includes a built-in REST API that's backwards compatible with Home Assistant's Fully Kiosk Browser integration. If you're migrating from Fully Kiosk, your existing automations and scripts will work out of the box — no reconfiguration needed.
